What is luxury pret?

Luxury pret is designer ready-to-wear sold as a finished, stitched outfit. It uses finer fabric, heavier embroidery and cleaner finishing than everyday pret, but less handwork than formals or bridal couture. Most luxury pret arrives as a shirt, trouser and dupatta designed together, so you do not source or tailor separate pieces.

The usual fabrics are chiffon, organza, silk and velvet. The designer decides everything together: neckline, sleeves, where the embroidery sits, shirt length, trousers. That is why a set needs so little styling.

An outfit reaches a UK buyer one of two ways. It ships from stock in a standard size, or the stockist stitches it to your measurements first. The route sets the wait and the return terms, so find out which one you are buying. Our ready-to-wear vs made-to-measure Pakistani dresses guide untangles the two.

How is luxury pret different from formals and bridal wear?

Luxury pret is lighter, quicker to get and less heavily embellished than formals or bridal, but finer than everyday pret. The five categories differ in embellishment, alteration margin and planning time. Everyday pret suits daytime, luxury pret suits dinners and guest events, formals suit wedding events, and bridal is for the bride.

Is luxury pret formal enough for a UK wedding guest?

Yes, luxury pret is formal enough for most wedding events a guest attends, including the nikkah, mehndi and walima. Formality comes from the colour and the embroidery, not the label. How does luxury pret fit?

Silk luxury pret often fits closer than everyday pret, so we suggest going up around one size. Size labels differ between Pakistani brands, and some brands list the wearer's measurements while others list the garment's. Measure your bust, waist and hips in inches and compare them with that brand's own size chart before you order.

If your measurements fall across two or more sizes, do not gamble on a standard size. Heavy embroidery leaves a tailor little room to fix a wrong guess. Have the outfit stitched to your measurements instead. Can luxury pret be altered, and what does it cost in the UK?

Most luxury pret can be shortened, taken in or let out slightly, and a UK tailor charges from about £7 for a simple hem to over £120 for complex work. Embroidered seams and borders limit how much a tailor can change. Choosing the right size at order is cheaper than altering after delivery.

How do you check a luxury pret stockist is authentic?

Check that the stockist names the designer on each product, that the outfit arrives with the designer's tags and packaging, and that the stockist states which brands it is authorised for. Authorisation is brand specific, so a stockist authorised for one designer is not automatically authorised for another.
