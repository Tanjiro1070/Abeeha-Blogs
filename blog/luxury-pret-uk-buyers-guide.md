## What is luxury pret?

**Luxury pret is designer ready-to-wear sold as a finished, stitched outfit.** It uses finer fabric, heavier embroidery and cleaner finishing than everyday pret, but less handwork than formals or bridal couture. Most luxury pret arrives as a shirt, trouser and dupatta designed together, so you do not source or tailor separate pieces.

The usual fabrics are chiffon, organza, silk and velvet. The designer decides everything together: neckline, sleeves, where the embroidery sits, shirt length, trousers. That is why a set needs so little styling.

An outfit reaches a UK buyer one of two ways. It ships from stock in a standard size, or the stockist stitches it to your measurements first. The route sets the wait and the return terms, so find out which one you are buying. ## How is luxury pret different from formals and bridal wear?

**Luxury pret is lighter, quicker to get and less heavily embellished than formals or bridal, but finer than everyday pret.** The five categories differ in embellishment, alteration margin and planning time. Everyday pret suits daytime, luxury pret suits dinners and guest events, formals suit wedding events, and bridal is for the bride.

| Category | Typical look | Best for | Alteration margin | Planning time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Everyday pret | Lawn or cotton, prints, light embroidery | Daytime, work, casual Eid visits | Wide | Short |
| Luxury pret | Chiffon, organza, silk or velvet with embroidery and lined finishing | Dinners, engagements, Eid, guest wear at nikkah, mehndi and walima | Moderate | Moderate |
| Formals | Heavier embellishment, often stitched to your measurements | Wedding guests, including the baraat | Limited | Longer |
| Bridal | Heaviest handwork and structured construction | The bride | Hardest to alter | Longest, so order weeks ahead |
| Unstitched | Fabric only, with embroidered panels where the design needs them | Buyers with a trusted tailor | Set by your tailor | Your tailor's schedule |

Embroidery is what limits alteration. Heavy borders and structured fabrics are hard to let out once they are stitched, so the margin shrinks as you move from pret towards bridal. ## How does luxury pret fit?

**Silk luxury pret often fits closer than everyday pret, so we suggest going up around one size.** Size labels differ between Pakistani brands, and some brands list the wearer's measurements while others list the garment's. Measure your bust, waist and hips in inches and compare them with that brand's own size chart before you order.

If your measurements fall across two or more sizes, do not gamble on a standard size. Heavy embroidery leaves a tailor little room to fix a wrong guess.
